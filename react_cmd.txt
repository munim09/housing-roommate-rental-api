Screen Shot of Full Webpage

1. Using a Web Browser
Google Chrome / Edge / Firefox:
Open the webpage you want to capture.
Press Ctrl + Shift + I (Windows) or Cmd + Option + I (Mac) to open Developer Tools.
Press Ctrl + Shift + P (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + P (Mac) to open the Command Menu.
Type “screenshot” and select:
Capture full size screenshot (captures the entire page, not just visible area).
The screenshot will download automatically as a PNG.
